Hey Tiny Guests

We're excited to frolic with you at Tiny Fest! Please read this carefully, it'll help you have the best possible time at our renegade forest party.

What to know

  • While it's only 25 minutes from downtown Oakland, there is no cell reception at the site. We will have limited wifi in case of emergencies. Be prepared to unplug. :)
  • There is water on site. We will also have non-alc beverages and pizza for sale. You are welcome to bring whatever makes you happy beyond these essentials.
  • There are bathrooms on site.
  • There is parking on site. Please park in the designated area to avoid ticketing and park close so we can fit as many cars as possible. We recommend carpooling. See directions for the full route in.

What to bring

  • Water bottle
  • Cash for food and tiny merch
  • Snacks and food to share
  • Flashlight or headlamp
  • Layers
  • Camping gear if you're spending the night
  • Anything else to feel comfortable (picnic blanket, folding chair, yoga mat for Sunday sound bath, etc.)

Before you go

Please review our community agreements and sign this waiver prior to coming to the festival so your entry is fast and smooth.

Finding your way in

Tiny Fest sits off-grid — no mains power, no cell service, and the last stretch isn't paved. Here's the route: how to get there, where to park, and what check-in looks like.

Gates open 3:00 PM
Address Bort Meadow Staging Area
01

The address

Punch the address below into your maps app before you lose signal — the last stretch is where GPS tends to give up.

Bort Meadow Staging Area, Redwood Rd, Oakland, CA 94619

At the staging area: don't pull into the main day-use parking lot straight ahead — that's public trailhead parking, not ours. Instead, look for the gate just to the left of the lot entrance. It'll be unlocked — go on through and onto the dirt road.

02

Parking

Follow the dirt road in from the gate until you hit another dirt road crossing it — a perpendicular T. Turn left there and you'll find the parking lot. Park up, then walk to the far end of the lot — that's where the path into the festival grounds picks up.

  • Carpool is highly encouraged — the lot is limited and every car you can combine helps keep it that way for everyone.
  • It's a dirt lot — nothing exotic needed, just expect dust and the odd rut, not pavement.
03

Check-in

Doors open at 3:00 PM — come any time after that, there's no cutoff.

From the far end of the lot, head into the trees toward the noise and light (once it's dark, you won't miss it) — a short walk through the woods opens onto the festival meadow.

The check-in table sits right at the meadow entrance, where the walk-in path opens up. Volunteers there will greet you — your name and/or email is enough to get checked in. Have your ticket or confirmation handy if you've got it (from Secret Party), but name/email works either way.

Community Agreements

By attending Tiny Fest, we agree to the following community agreements.

  • We will respect each other and keep each other safe.
  • Racism, misogyny, transphobia, homophobia, and xenophobia will not be tolerated.
  • We will honor consent practices on and off the dance floor. That means asking for permission to enter a person's physical and emotional space. And respecting the answer.
  • We will practice safe consumption and will hold ourselves accountable to not overly consume substances that could affect our ability to navigate the event safely and respectfully.
  • We will respect the space by picking up after ourselves, using the provided trash and recycling bins, and following a "leave no trace" ethos.
  • We will park in designated areas. And we will come and go from the event in a safe manner.
  • We will respect and celebrate the performers and contributing artists and will engage with the installations and art respectfully.

If you run into issues, please go to the help desk area to alert a volunteer. There will also be walkie talkies at each area of the event if you need to reach someone remotely.
